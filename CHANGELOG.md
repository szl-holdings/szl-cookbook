## v14 math corrections (2026-05-28, fix/v14-math-corrections)

Tutorial-fixture math corrections in `recipes/anatomy-evolved-v1/code/`.

### `src/amaru-qkan-fwp.ts` — DARUAN Rx/Rz unitarity (Fix 1)

The prior `rx()` and `rz()` helpers in the cookbook recipe applied
non-unitary rotation matrices: the `rx` row coefficients had a sign
error that broke ⟨ψ|ψ⟩ preservation. Corrected to the standard
Nielsen-Chuang single-qubit rotations:

  R_x(θ) = cos(θ/2)·I − i·sin(θ/2)·X
  R_z(φ) = diag(e^{−iφ/2}, e^{iφ/2})

(Nielsen & Chuang, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*,
Cambridge UP, 2010, §4.2 eq. 4.4–4.5.) Added explicit named exports
`rx`, `rz`, `QubitState`.

**New test:** `tests/rx_rz_unitarity.ts` — 100 random (θ, φ, |ψ⟩) draws
asserting ‖U|ψ⟩‖ − 1 < 1e-10, round-trip inverses, and Rx(2π) = −I.
Passes locally.

### `src/a11oy-ks18-witness.ts` — KS-18 contexts (Fix 2)

The prior `KS18_VECTORS` and `KS18_CONTEXTS` constants used 3-component
triples with 3-vector contexts. The KS-18 construction lives in ℝ⁴:
18 rays with entries in {-1, 0, +1}^4, partitioned into 9 contexts of
4 mutually orthogonal rays, every ray appearing in exactly 2 contexts.
The 9 contexts were generated by brute-force search over the
Peres-24-ray parent set in ℝ⁴ until orthogonality and 2-incidence were
satisfied.

Sources:
- Cabello, A., Estebaranz, J. M., & García-Alcaine, G. (1996).
  "Bell-Kochen-Specker theorem: A proof with 18 vectors."
  *Physics Letters A* 212(4), 183–187. arXiv:quant-ph/9706009.
- Peres, A. (1991). "Two simple proofs of the Kochen-Specker theorem."
  *J. Phys. A: Math. Gen.* 24, L175.

Added `verifyKSStructure()` which checks (i) every context's 4 vectors
are pairwise orthogonal and (ii) every vector appears in exactly 2
contexts. The Cabello parity proof of NCHV impossibility now follows
directly from these invariants.

**New test:** `tests/ks18_structure.ts` — verifies structure, the
parity argument, and the CLASSICAL / inconsistency flag transitions.
Passes locally.

### `lean/TwoWitness.lean` — stub pointing at canonical (Fix 3)

The prior cookbook copy proved a Metatron fixed-point theorem unrelated
to KS-18 contextuality, contradicting the claim in
`docs/v14/ch9_anatomy_evolved_v1.md` §9.2.2. Replaced with a stub
pointing to the canonical lutar-lean module:

  `lutar-lean/Lutar/TwoWitness.lean`

which actually states and proves the soundness direction (no sorry)
and structurally encodes the Cabello parity hardness (`no_NCHV`,
one tagged `sorry` on the routine `Finset.sum_bij` double-counting
step).
